Purpose: Help shoppers find tires that fit their vehicle and are available for installation at a selected warehouse at their preferred time. 
Goals: 
#Experience GoalsBusiness Impact1Native Mobile app experience for tire selection and appointment bookingEngagement rate, Click through rate, conversion rate2Consistent experience across costco.com/ca web, mobile and other productsLower exit/drop-off, bounce rate
Returning customer volume increases3Preserve shopper context across touchpointsCLV4Eliminate flows that redirect shoppers into any disconnected experience (taking them away from scheduling/service)Improved add to cart/checkout
User Personas
- Loyalist
- Repeat Shopper
- Intent based: Tire Search, Global search, Browse and navigate, Deal first, Service first (install/rotate/flat)

Functional 
- System must validate the tire size format and match customer requirement
- Warehouse availability and timeline for delivery to the warehouse must be accurate
- Available appointment times must be displayed to the shopper during the tire selection process
- Warehouse specific inventory must be indicated to the shopper (along with clear/quicker fulfillment dates)
- No case of appointment cancellations after a shopper has already scheduled
- 
Non-Functional Requirements
- Performance must be fast with load times of <=2 seconds
- 99.99% uptime on the app and website
- No instance of data loss or any such security issues
- Ability for the application to scale real-time to support high volumes during any peak period or campaign driven spikes
- Inclusive experience on the app and website for all shoppers through accessibility features
- Compliance from a legal and regulatory standpoint
