The goal of this project is to modernize the automotive member experience by replacing a fragmented digital journey with a high-performance, unified ecosystem designed to capture untapped market share. By re-envisioning the user journey based on behavioral research and industry standards, we aim to significantly reduce digital drop-offs and strengthen our credibility with the next generation of shoppers, particularly within the growing EV segment. This transformation centers on a "native-first" mobile strategy, leveraging the Costco mobile app's native UI and APIs to drive higher conversion rates and increase the frequency of repeat purchases.
To achieve these objectives, we are systematically eliminating the friction points that currently lead to site abandonment. By providing real-time Inventory Availability for tires across local and regional warehouses, we ensure members have immediate visibility into product stock. Integrating an Installation Scheduler directly into the selection flow further optimizes club operations by improving tire installation bay utilization and reducing fragmented scheduling. Furthermore, by standardizing the Battery Sales experience, we provide a consistent, high-quality buying experience across all geographic regions.
Beyond the transaction, we are investing in high-leverage content and member personalization to foster long-term loyalty. Implementing 3rd Party guides and specialized content pages (organized by driving needs, season, and category) empowers members to make informed decisions with minimal friction. Finally, by allowing members to store vehicles and purchase history on their profiles, we simplify the path to repurchase and rebate redemption, delivering the seamless value and convenience that defines the Costco member experience.


SCOPE.
Deliver a single, consistent tire-buying journey across Costco.com & .ca and the Costco mobile app, so members can start in any channel and complete in any channel—with fitment, pricing, and scheduling context preserved end-to-end. The experience must eliminate re-entry of information and prevent members from being redirected into disconnected scheduling or service flows.

Phased Delivery Plan:
Phase 1 (A), Tires Journey Reimagine: This phase offers a clean experience for tire selection. This update improves the shopping experience and supports future enhancements. At tires.costco.com/.ca, we aim to reduce purchase friction and abandonment by simplifying user journeys. This foundational work builds digital trust and navigation, boosting conversions. This approach will change how customers find the tires they need!
Phase 1 (B), Azure to GCP Migration: This phase is our attempt to modernize the hosting platform to bring the tires application to Costco preferred cloud provider services. For complete scope refer to Infogain - Costco-Tires-Azure-To-GCP Migration-V5.pdf in the tires folder
Phase 2, Tires Mobile App: Costco Wholesale’s mobile apps (iOS & Android) transitioned from hybrid to native, resulting in significantly better user experience and engagement. Currently, the Costco Tire section lacks a native interface, offering only a link to the web version. This initiative will build a fully native tire shopping experience within the existing app, covering navigation, marketing & educational content, product listing and detail pages, search, filters, and additional content. The native tire experience will integrate features like single sign-on, profile, cart, and checkout. This phase aims to deliver an engaging, seamless purchase journey for members, including enhanced cross-sell and upsell opportunities.
